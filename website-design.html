    What’s offered

    New sites, and a fresh look for existing ones.

    Whether you’re starting from scratch or your current site is dated, slow or awkward on a phone, we can help. This is for any small business, not only hotels and guesthouses.

    New website builds

    A clear, modern site for your business, on your own domain, with the pages you need — about your work, how to find you, and how customers get in touch or book.

    Website redesigns

    A fresh, modern look and a clearer layout for your current site, keeping your content, photos and domain. Your site can stay where it is, or move to us to host.

    See Website redesign in the services list

    How it works

    A straightforward process, agreed with you at each step.

    1. We look at what you have, and what you need.If you already have a site, we review it with you. If you don’t, we talk through the pages and features your business needs.
    2. We agree the pages, look and content.You decide the tone, colours and photographs. We set out the structure so it is clear what goes on the site before we build it.
    3. We build or redesign the site, and refine it with you.You see the work as it takes shape, and we adjust the layout and wording with you before anything goes live.
    4. We go live on your domain.The site is published on your own address, with hosting and a secure (HTTPS) certificate, so visitors see the padlock.
    5. We can keep looking after it.Updates, renewals and day-to-day care can stay with us afterwards, so the site does not quietly fall behind.
